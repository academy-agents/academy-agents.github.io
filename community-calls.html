Anyone in the Academy Slack workspace is welcome to join. wide: true --- {%- comment -%} The next call and the archive are split at BUILD TIME, on whole days, so a call reads as upcoming for the whole of its own day rather than flipping to "past" at midnight UTC while people are still dialling in. `site.time` is the build clock, so this is only as fresh as the last deploy — see the note at the top of _data/community_calls.yml. Past calls reuse .post-row from the blog index rather than getting their own grid: same four columns, same responsive collapse, no new CSS.
