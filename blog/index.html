APeX Summit (Academy, Parsl, Globus Compute) annual community meeting

The APeX Summit (Academy, Parsl, Globus Compute) is the eighth annual community meeting, bringing together researchers, developers, and cyberinfrastructure experts worldwide to discuss experiences using and developing Academy, Parsl, and Globus Compute. The summit will be held as a hybrid meeting on September 14–15, 2026, with the in-person component in Chicago. Full details, including the venue and program, are coming soon.
